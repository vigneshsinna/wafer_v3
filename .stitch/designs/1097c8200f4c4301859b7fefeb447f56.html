Ingredients & Sourcing

Karuppu Kavuni is an ancient heirloom grain native to Tamil Nadu, revered since the Chola dynasty. Unlike regular polished rice, its deep purple-black hue comes from dense concentrations of anthocyanins — powerful antioxidants with 6x the potency of blueberries. It has a low glycemic index (GI 42), slow-release energy, and high dietary fiber with zero chemical processing.

verified Direct farm harvest contracts with organic cultivators in the Kaveri river basin.

Every botanical ingredient is wildcrafted or organically farmed across Tamil Nadu. Our golden Avarampoo (Senna auriculata) is gathered from dry rural tracts around Gobichettipalayam, Hibiscus calyces come from organic family farms near Bhavani, and brain-tonic Vallarai (Centella asiatica) is handpicked along Kaveri delta wetlands.

pin_drop Erode Hub: 100% trace-tagged within a 65 km radius of our stone bakery.

Absolute zero palm oil, zero trans fats, and zero hydrogenated oils. We lightly mist our wafers with cold-pressed gingelly (sesame) and wood-churned groundnut oils before controlled oven-crisping. Zero artificial preservatives, chemical colors, or synthetic MSG.

Packaging & Shelf Life

6 months from the date of stone-baking. Each pack is nitrogen-flushed and triple-barrier sealed with food-grade foil to preserve crispness without chemical additives.

While our resealable zip pouch protects freshness, we recommend enjoying within 5–7 days of opening, or transferring to an airtight glass or tin jar stored in a cool, dry pantry away from direct sunlight.
